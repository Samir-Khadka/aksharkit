Normalise

Text that looks the same can be stored in different ways, which breaks search. This gives one canonical form: repaired vowels, tidy spaces, । for a typed |.

Tokenise

Split text into words and sentences. Nepali sentences end in ।, and a full stop inside डा. or रु. 99.50 must not end one.

      Transliterate

      Devanagari to Roman letters and back. IAST uses diacritics and is exact in both directions. Simple is plain ASCII, the way Nepali is typed in chat; it is rule-based and approximate.

      Preeti to Unicode

      Coming soon — needs a verified mapping. A great deal of older Nepali text is stored in the Preeti font. aksharkit will not guess the key-to-letter table, and the tables published so far are either under licences an MIT project cannot copy or do not say where they came from.

      What unblocks it: real Preeti text with the correct Unicode beside it, to test against. If you have some, please open an issue on GitHub.
